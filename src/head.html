CONTROLS & KIT
Stick (left): steer sideways, push up to burn, pull down to vent. FIRE (hold): Repeater. ROCKET / FLAK: fire that weapon now. BOOST dashes along the stick direction. Cooldown sweeps fill clockwise; red text means not enough fuel.
Weapons
Repeater — twin-barrel deck gun, fires dead level. Hold to stream tracers. No fuel cost, but every shot builds heat; hit 100 and the guns lock until they cool. Tracers can shoot down rockets, flak shells and bombs.
Seeker Rocket — drops from its rail, ignites, then steers with lead toward your rival. 2 in the rack, slow reload. Shoot-down-able.
Flak Mortar — lobbed shell with a proximity fuse that bursts at its closest pass. Heavy damage, one in the tube. Great from above.
Drop Bomb — release a weighted bomb straight down. Shedding the weight gives you a burst of lift.
Abilities
Harpoon — chain-hook that tethers your rival and reels them in for 1.4s, disrupting their steering and breaking any patch. Follow with a Shockwave.
Vent Shockwave — short-fused blast around you. Hurts anyone close, releases your tether, and swats enemy ordnance and hook lines out of the sky.
Aegis Shield — absorbs 80% of damage for 2s. Long cooldown, real fuel cost.
Patch Kit — channel 1.3s to restore hull. You can't steer or burn while patching, and a solid hit (or a harpoon) breaks it. Shield first if you're under pressure.
Flare — drop a slow-burning flare that lights up the fog around it, revealing any pilot hiding nearby for several seconds.
Gas Lamps — hover beside a lit lamp to refuel from its gas main (it runs dry if you camp). Its light gives away anyone near it. Shoot the glass and the gas bursts — dangerous to anyone close.
Wind — every altitude layer blows its own way and changes over time; climb or drop to steer. Gusts are announced a few seconds ahead. The two warm columns (rising sparks) give free lift.
Afterburner Boost — the basket thrusters dump fuel for a hard dash in your steering direction (add up/down for an angled dash). Snaps any hook line on you.
Esc reopens this menu at any time. F3 shows what each Wind Pilot is thinking.
